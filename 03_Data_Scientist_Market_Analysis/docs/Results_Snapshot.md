# Results Snapshot

## Headline

- Python remained the most consistently requested tool across role types in the 2021 posting sample.
- California, Massachusetts, and New York showed the strongest concentration of jobs and salary ranges.
- Degree requirements and compensation expectations tended to increase with role seniority.

## Why it matters

| Question | Takeaway |
|---|---|
| What does this say about labor demand? | Employer demand clustered geographically and rewarded a familiar technical stack rather than a long tail of tools. |
| How is it useful for people analytics? | It translates external labor-market data into workforce planning, talent-supply, and skills-demand signals. |
| What is the caution? | Public job-posting data should be treated as directional, not as a definitive compensation or hiring benchmark. |
